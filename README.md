---

### 🏇 **Overview**
**Spirit: Stallion of the Cimarron** is a **2002 animated adventure film** by **DreamWorks Animation**.  
It tells the powerful story of **Spirit**, a wild mustang stallion who fights to remain free while exploring friendship, courage, and the beauty of nature.  

🎵 *Featuring music by Bryan Adams and score by Hans Zimmer.*

---

### 🐴 **Main Characters**
| Character | Description |
|:-----------|:-------------|
| **Spirit** | A courageous wild mustang determined to stay free. |
| **Rain** | A beautiful and gentle mare who steals Spirit’s heart. ❤️ |
| **Little Creek** | A brave Lakota warrior who befriends Spirit. 🪶 |
| **The Colonel** | A determined U.S. officer who wants to tame Spirit. ⚔️ |
